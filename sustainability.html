Growers and forests

Sourced with Kindred Forest

We source our green coffee with Kindred Forest, a company that works directly with Indonesian coffee communities. Its focus is traceability, growers' livelihoods and protecting Indonesia's coffee forests.

Kindred Forest sources our beans; it doesn't roast them. Our beans are roasted by Bija's baristas.

At the bar

Less waste, every day

Free coffee grounds

Our used coffee grounds are free for your plants and garden. Just ask at the bar. We gave bags away for Earth Day, and the offer stands every day.

Bring your own cup

We're planning 25p off when you bring a clean reusable cup. Ask the team whether it has started yet.

Planned deliveries

The Beans Club sends coffee on a schedule you choose, every 2, 3 or 4 weeks, so bags arrive when you need them rather than in rushed one-off orders.

Fresh, so nothing's wasted

The Bean passport shows each bag's roast date and the best days to drink it, so less coffee goes stale in the cupboard.

Community

Coffee that gives back

For Indonesian Independence Day we celebrated with Edge Group Conservation, raising support for the Javan rhino, one of the world's rarest animals. We'll keep using our events to support Indonesian conservation.

Our claims, plainly

What we say, and how we back it up

We follow the UK Competition and Markets Authority's Green Claims Code: claims must be truthful, clear, complete and backed by evidence.

  • We say

    Our green coffee is sourced with Kindred Forest. We keep our sourcing records and partner agreements on file.

  • We don't say

    "Carbon neutral", "eco-friendly", "compostable" or anything else we can't prove. You won't see us claim them.

  • Ask us

    Want the evidence behind anything on this page? Email info@bijalondon.co.uk and we'll share what we have.
