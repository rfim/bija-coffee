Good to know

Before you come

Order ahead

Skip the queue: order in the app, pick a time, and collect at the bar. You earn points either way.

Allergens

Tell us about allergies before you order. We can show you allergen information for every drink and cake, including the 14 major allergens.

Access

St James's Market is a pedestrian square. If you need step-free access or a particular seat, call us before you visit and we'll help.

Bring a cup

Bring your own reusable cup and we'll happily fill it. Ask at the bar about the current offer.

Coffee grounds

Used coffee grounds are free for your plants and garden. Just ask the team.

Beans to go

200 g bags of every coffee we pour, ground to order at the bar, or delivered to your door.
