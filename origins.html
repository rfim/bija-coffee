From the islands

Where our kopi grows.

Indonesia has more than 17,000 islands. Our coffees come from the volcanic highlands of three of them: Sumatra, Java and Bali. Tap a pin to meet each one.

Map data: Natural Earth (public domain)

Wet-hulled

Most Sumatran coffee is giling basah: hulled while still damp. It's why Sumatra tastes heavy, earthy and low in acidity.

Aged Java

Old Brown Java is rested for years before roasting, so it turns mellow, woody and gently spiced.

Volcanic soil

The highlands around Lake Toba, Gayo and Mount Batur sit at 1,000 to 1,600 metres on rich volcanic soil.
