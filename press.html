About Bija, for publication

Bija is an Indonesian specialty coffee shop at 2a St James's Market, London, founded by Devi Trianna. Named Best New Coffee Shop at the London Coffee Festival Awards 2025, Bija serves coffees from Sumatra and Java, roasted with partner Kindred Forest, alongside kopi susu with arenga palm sugar, kopi tubruk and lapis legit. Find Bija on Instagram at @bija.london.
