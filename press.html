About Bija

Use as is, or shorten.

Bija is an independent Indonesian specialty coffee shop at St James's Market, London, founded by Devi Trianna. It serves Indonesian coffee and food the way they're enjoyed at home, from kopi susu with arenga palm sugar to kopi tubruk and lapis legit, and sells Indonesian beans to brew at home, roasted by its own baristas.

Bija was named Best New Coffee Shop at the London Coffee Festival Awards 2025.
