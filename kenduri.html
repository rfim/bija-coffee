The tradition

What is a kenduri?

A kenduri is a communal feast from Java and the wider Malay world. Neighbours, family and friends sit together to share food, give thanks and mark an occasion: a harvest, a new home, a birth, a journey, or simply the wish that a community stays close.

Many kenduri are centred on a tumpeng, a cone of rice (often yellow nasi kuning, coloured with turmeric) set on a round tray and surrounded by side dishes such as vegetables with spiced coconut, egg, tempeh and chicken. The shape echoes a mountain, and the first portion from the top is traditionally offered to the eldest or most honoured guest.

For many families a kenduri also has a spiritual side, with prayers or blessings that are part of their faith and custom. Ours is a shared supper inspired by that spirit of togetherness and gratitude. We won't recreate a religious ceremony, and we'll be guided by the Indonesian community we cook for.

What to expect

    A shared long table

    Everyone sits together, strangers included. Dishes are set down the middle to pass and share, family style.

    Coffee to match

    Each course paired with Indonesian coffee from our origins, from a bright pour-over to a sweet kopi susu aren to close.
