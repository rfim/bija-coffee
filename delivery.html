We deliver to UK addresses only. Our coffee is sourced with our partner Kindred Forest and roasted by Bija's baristas, and we usually dispatch within two working days. You'll get an email with tracking when your order leaves us. Prices include VAT.

Click & collect

Choose click & collect at checkout and collect from Bija, 2a St James's Market, London SW1Y 4AH, during opening hours (Monday to Friday 8am to 5.30pm, Saturday 11am to 6pm, Sunday 12pm to 6pm). We'll email you when it's ready. Show your order code at the bar.

Returns and your right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive it. Email info@bijalondon.co.uk with your order code to tell us.

  • Send the goods back, or bring them to the café, within 14 days of telling us. Bags must be unopened and still sealed.
  • We refund the price and the standard delivery cost within 14 days of getting the goods back. You pay the cost of sending them back.
  • Coffee is food and goes stale once opened. For hygiene reasons we can't take back bags that have been opened, unless they are faulty.

Something wrong with your order?

If a bag arrives damaged, the wrong coffee or not as described, tell us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you in full, including any return postage. This is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions have no minimum term. Change, skip, pause or cancel any time up to 3 days before your next dispatch, in the Bija app, by email or at the bar. You're charged on the day each delivery is dispatched. Your 14-day right to cancel applies to each delivery as above.

Gift subscriptions are paid once for three deliveries and don't renew.
