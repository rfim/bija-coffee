You're all set

You're already using the Bija app from your home screen.

Open the app

Your browser can add Bija straight to your home screen.

  1. Open in Safari

    Open the Bija app in Safari. Other iPhone browsers can't add apps to the home screen.

  2. Tap Share

    Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll the menu, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Bija appears with your other apps.

  1. Open in Chrome

    Open the Bija app in Chrome. If a bar offers to install Bija, tap it and you're done.

  2. Open the menu

    Tap the three dots in the top-right corner of Chrome.

  3. Install app

    Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen), then Install.
