Questions

Subscription FAQs

Is there a minimum term?
No. You can cancel after your first delivery, or before it ships.
How do I change, skip, pause or cancel?
Change, skip, pause or cancel any time up to 3 days before your next dispatch. Do it in the Bija app under Beans Club, email info@bijalondon.co.uk, or ask at the bar.
When will I be charged?
On the day each delivery is dispatched, never in advance. In this demo no payment is taken.
Can I change my coffee or grind?
Yes, for any delivery that hasn't shipped yet. Try a different island each time if you like.
Do subscriptions earn points?
Yes: 10 points for every £1, landing on your Bija Card when each delivery is dispatched. How rewards work.
Where do you deliver?
UK addresses by Royal Mail Tracked 48, free on every subscription. You can also collect from 2a St James's Market. See delivery and returns.
