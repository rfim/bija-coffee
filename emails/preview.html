Email templates

Five transactional and marketing emails: 600px table layouts with inline CSS, light and dark mode, and a plain-text version of each. Fields in {{double_braces}} are merge tags; {{#items}} repeats the line for each item. Use en-GB dates such as 29 October 2026 and prices including VAT.

    Sent when an order is placed: items, totals, delivery address and points.
