Orders and collection

How do I order ahead?

Open the Bija app, tap the round Order button, choose your drinks and how you like them, pick a time and place your order. You pay at the bar when you collect.

How do I know when my order is ready?

Your order shows live in the app, in Profile: received, brewing, then ready. When it says ready, show your order code at the bar.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Tell our team at the bar as soon as you can. If we haven't started making it, we'll happily change or cancel it.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8am to 5.30pm, Saturday 11am to 6pm, Sunday 12pm to 6pm, at 2a St James's Market, London SW1Y 4AH. See Contact.

Beans Club

What is the Beans Club?

A coffee subscription: choose a bean and grind, and we send a 200 g bag every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. You save 10% on every bag (£10.80 instead of £12) and delivery is free.

Can I skip, pause or cancel?

Yes, any time up to 3 days before your next dispatch, with no minimum term. Manage it in the app under Profile, then Beans Club: skip the next delivery, pause, change your grind or how often, or cancel.

When will my next bag arrive?

The app shows your next delivery date. Bags go out with Royal Mail Tracked 48, which usually arrives within 2 working days of dispatch.

Delivery and returns

How much is delivery?

Royal Mail Tracked 48 is £3.95, and free on orders of £30 or more or with any subscription. Click and collect at 2a St James's Market is always free.

Can I return my coffee?

You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order and return unopened goods for a refund. For hygiene reasons we can't take back opened coffee unless it's faulty. Full details are in Delivery and returns.

My order arrived damaged or wrong. What now?

We're sorry. Email info@bijalondon.co.uk with your order code and a photo, and we'll put it right with a replacement or a refund.

Bija Card points and stamps

How do I earn points?

You earn 10 points for every £1 you spend in the café, in the app and in the web shop. Points land once our team marks your order collected or dispatched, or scans your card at the till.

What can I spend points on?

Any cake for 350 points, or a 200 g bag of beans for 1,200 points. Choose your reward at checkout.

How do stamps work?

You get 1 stamp for every handcrafted drink. Collect 9 and your 10th drink is free, up to £5.50. Find your stamp card in the app under Profile.

What are Seed, Sprout and Kopi Tree?

Your Bija Card level grows as you earn. Everyone starts at Seed. Sprout (1,500 points earned) adds a free extra shot or syrup on any drink, and Kopi Tree (5,000 points earned) brings first tastes of new origins and invites to Bija events.

How do invites work?

Share your invite code from the app. When a friend joins with it and collects their first order, you each get 300 points once our team confirms it.

Gift cards

How do gift cards work?

Choose an amount from £5, pick a design and add a message in the app. Once paid, the recipient adds the code in their app and spends it on drinks, cakes or beans.

Can I send a coffee subscription as a gift?

Yes: the gift subscription is three 200 g bags over three months, one a month, prepaid at £36 with delivery included.

Bean passport QR

What is the QR code on my bag or cup?

Scan it with your phone camera to open the Bean passport: the region, altitude, process, tasting notes, roast date, how fresh it is and how to brew it.

Why don't I see the farm name?

We only show farm and lot details when Bija has published them for that batch. We'd rather leave a gap than guess.

Can I scan inside the app?

Yes, tap Scan coffee. On iPhone, if scanning inside the app isn't available, use your Camera app instead. The camera is only used to read the code: nothing is recorded or sent to us.

App and install

How do I install the app?

Open the app on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install in the prompt or in the Chrome menu.

Do I need an account?

No email or password is needed to order ahead. Your Bija Card is created when you first open the app.

Accounts and privacy

What data do you keep?

Only what we need to make your order and run your Bija Card, plus your email if you choose to get our news. Everything is explained in our privacy notice.

Do you use cookies?

This site sets no cookies. Your browser stores a few things, like your basket, so the site works. See the privacy notice.

How do I get a copy of my data or delete it?

Email info@bijalondon.co.uk. We'll reply within one month.

Is the site accessible?

We design and test to WCAG 2.2 AA. Read our accessibility statement, or tell us about a problem and we'll fix it.

Allergens

Do you have allergen information?

Yes. Please tell our team about any allergy before you order and we'll show you the full information for every drink and cake. See Allergens.

Which milks do you offer?

Whole, oat, soya and coconut, at no extra cost.
