Accessibility

Last updated: 29 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to find our hours, read our menu, order ahead and use their Bija Card. We design and test the website and app against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do

  • Zoom the page up to 200% without losing content.
  • Move through the website and app with a keyboard, and skip straight to the main content.
  • Use a screen reader: photos have descriptions and buttons have labels.
  • Turn off movement: if your device is set to reduce motion, our animations stop.
  • Order without the app: our team will always take your order and add points at the till.

How we check

We last checked the website on 29 October 2026: a keyboard-only walk through every link, button and pop-up, a check that every button and link has a name a screen reader can read, contrast checks on every text colour, and tests on a large desktop screen and an iPhone-sized screen. We test on recent iPhone, Android and desktop browsers.

Known issues

  • The map on our website is a simple drawing. The address, opening hours and a link to directions sit right next to it.
  • The founder's photo in the About section is still to come; until then you'll see a labelled placeholder.

If something doesn't work for you, it's a bug we want to fix.
