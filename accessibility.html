Accessibility

Last updated: 29 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to find our hours, read our menu, order ahead and use their Bija Card. We design and test the website and app against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do

  • Zoom the page up to 200% without losing content.
  • Move through the website and app with a keyboard, and skip straight to the main content.
  • Use a screen reader: photos have descriptions and buttons have labels.
  • Turn off movement: if your device is set to reduce motion, our animations stop.
  • Order without the app: our team will always take your order and add points at the till.

How we test

Every page of the website and every screen of the app is checked with the axe-core accessibility engine against WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules, at desktop and phone sizes, each time we change them. We also check by hand: keyboard only, 200% zoom, reduced motion, and screen reader labels on buttons, photos and maps.

Conformance status

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA. As far as we know the website and app meet it, with the known issues below. This statement was prepared on 29 October 2026 and will be reviewed at least once a year.

Known issues

  • Maps of St James's and of Indonesia are drawings. Everything on them is also given in text next to the map: addresses, walking times and coffee regions.
  • The Bija Card QR code can't be read aloud. Your member code is printed under it, and the barista can type it in.
  • Brew timers count down visually. They also announce each step for screen readers, and you can pause them at any time.

We test on recent iPhone, Android and desktop browsers. If something doesn't work for you, it's a bug we want to fix.
