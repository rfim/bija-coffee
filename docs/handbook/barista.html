Behind the bar

1 · Staff mode
  1. Hold the round Order button for 3 seconds until the ring closes.
  2. Enter the 6-digit staff PIN.
  3. Tap Exit when you leave the tablet. It signs out by itself after 30 minutes.
2 · App orders
  1. New order → Start brewing.
  2. Drink ready → Ready · call code.
  3. Customer shows the code → Collected · add points.
  4. Charge what the screen says. Price check warning? Charge the menu price.
3 · Till sales
  1. Ask for their Bija Card (Profile tab) and member code, e.g. BIJA-KJHV6.
  2. Members: code, amount paid, number of drinks.
  3. Tap Add points.
Free drink

Every handcrafted drink is a stamp. At 9 stamps the 10th drink is free, up to £5.50, on the priciest drink. The app applies it; you don't need to work it out.

Wrong button?

Tap Undo on the order. Never add points twice to fix a mistake: tell the owner.

Gift cards

  1. Customer chooses the card in the app and pays you.
  2. Gift cards → Take payment → Paid · activate card, entering the amount taken.
  3. The code shows once; the buyer sees it in their app.
  4. Redeeming: the customer types the code in their app. Each code works once.

Beans orders

  1. Web & delivery → Orders to send.
  2. Post with Royal Mail Tracked 48 → Dispatched.
  3. Click & collect → Collected when handed over.

Offers at the till proposal to confirm

  • Own cup: 25p off any hot drink.
  • Students: 10% off with a valid student card.
  • Early bird: tick double points, Mon–Fri before 9am.

Busy rush?

In Queue, tap Pause orders. Customers see ordering is paused. Turn it back on when you've caught up.

Allergies

Show customers the allergen information; never guess. Same jug and steam wand for all milks; cakes may contain nuts, milk, gluten and egg.

Call the owner when
  • Points, stamps or a gift balance look wrong.
  • A gift code is refused but the customer insists it's paid.
  • App orders stop arriving for more than 10 minutes.
  • A tablet or phone with staff mode is lost.
  • Someone asks for their data, or to delete their account.
Never
  • Share the PIN or write it down here.
  • Type a gift code or member code for someone else.
  • Add points without a real sale.
