## 9. Review-request routine

**The rules (Google policy and UK consumer law, including the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024):**
- **Never** offer points, discounts, free drinks or prize draws for a review, not even for "honest" ones.
- **Ask everyone**, not only happy customers. Don't send unhappy customers somewhere else instead (Google calls that review gating).
- Staff, friends and family **must not** review the shop.
- Never write or buy reviews, and never ask people to change or remove one.

**Set up once**
1. In the profile, choose *Ask for reviews* and copy the short review link (`g.page/r/…/review`).
2. Make a QR code for that link. Put it on a small card by the till and on the bean bag label, with the words:
   > Enjoyed your coffee? We'd love to hear what you think on Google. It takes a minute and helps other people find us.
3. Add the link to the email signature and the Instagram link list.
4. App team: show a "Tell us on Google" link on the order-collected screen, at most once every 90 days per customer. No points for it.

**Every day (barista)**
- When a regular says something nice, or a first-time visitor says they'll come back, say:
  "Thank you! If you've got a minute, a Google review really helps a small shop like ours. The QR code is just there."
  Ask once and don't push.

**Every week (manager, about 15 minutes, Monday morning)**
- Reply to **every** new review within 2 working days, using the customer's first name and mentioning something specific from their visit. Sign off "Devi and the Bija team".
- Positive review template:
  > Thank you, [name]! So glad the [kopi susu aren / Lintong] hit the spot. See you next time you're near Piccadilly. Devi and the Bija team
- Negative review template (calm, no excuses, take it offline):
  > Thank you for telling us, [name], and I'm sorry we got [the thing] wrong. That's not the visit we want anyone to have. Could you email info@bijalondon.co.uk so we can put it right? Devi and the Bija team
- If a review breaks Google's rules (abuse, spam, or someone who was never a customer), report it in the profile and don't argue in public.

**Every month (owner, about 30 minutes)**
- Check the *Performance* page for searches, calls, direction requests and website clicks, and write them in a simple spreadsheet. Compare with last month.
- Check the hours, special hours, menu prices and products are still right.
- Add new photos and plan two posts.
