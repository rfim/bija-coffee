# /brag plan: Bija app launch (20 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

**What it is:** Bija's app for its Indonesian specialty coffee shop in St James's Market, London. You order ahead, earn points and stamps on a Bija Card, and get beans delivered on a subscription.
**Who it's for:** people near Piccadilly who want their kopi without the queue, and regulars who come back for the card and the beans.
**What sets it apart:** a real café's app with Indonesian coffee at its heart (Kopi Susu Aren, Sumatra beans). It's calm black-and-white design, and it orders, rewards and delivers in one place.
**Hook:** "Skip the queue." slammed word by word on the beat, on black.
**Visual identity:** Bija's own black and white, the four-circle Kawung mark, Archivo 900 uppercase display and Inter body (the app's own fonts), and warm grey `#efedea` panels.
**Tone:** `default`, punchy, clean and confident; soft transitions.
**Music:** 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s) in D major: soft four-on-the-floor kick, off-beat hats, a warm bass on D–Bm–G–A, a plucked arpeggio, and a pad under the reveal and outro. Effects are in key and sit under the music: soft ticks on taps, a filtered swell into each cut, and a two-note chime when the card flips. Every cut lands on a beat.
**Real UI:** the live app, recorded at the 428 phone standard. The clock is fixed to a weekday at 10:15, so the shop shows as open. No personal data: guest account, menu items only.
