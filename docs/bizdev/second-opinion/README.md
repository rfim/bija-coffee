# Second opinion on loyalty economics

Two analysts modelled Bija's loyalty and pricing proposals independently on 29 October 2026:

- `docs/bizdev/` (Business Development team): keep stamps and points; drop or move early bird; students off-peak only.
- `docs/bizdev/second-opinion/` (business analyst): keep points; **drop stamps as a second reward** (points plus stamps give back about 19% at menu prices) or show the stamp grid only as progress to a free drink; drop the student discount; move early bird to Mon–Fri 2–4pm.

Both agree on: referral +300 each only after the friend's first paid order of £3 or more; Beans Club free posting from 2 bags (or £2.50 for a single posted bag); gift subscription £36 to collect and £42 posted; delivery £3.95, free over £30.

The site and app currently build **both** points and stamps, as the owner asked. Which mix to launch with is an owner decision. All figures are assumptions until 8 weeks of till and app data replace them.
