# Selling Bija beans beyond the shop

*Sprint 2 · Business Development · draft for Bija to approve · 29 Oct 2026*

**Recommendation for the next 90 days:**
1. Sell beans **online from Bija's own app and website**, posted, using the same packs and postage as the subscription.
2. Open **2–3 local stockists** (delis, Indonesian and Southeast Asian grocers) as a test.
3. Offer **1 kg bags to offices** that already order catering.

Skip Amazon and Not On The High Street for now: they make the least per bag and take real effort. Etsy is a possible Christmas-only test.

> Bean cost is **£5.40 per 200 g bag** (45% of £12), the assumption from the brief. Numbers marked *(assumption)* have no source. **Before selling through anyone else, check Bija's agreement with Kindred Forest** (roasting partner) allows resale through third parties and under which name. **Prepacked food labelling, VAT and distance-selling rules: confirm with a food-law adviser, an accountant and a solicitor.**

## 2. Each option in more detail

### A. Own online shop (posted). **Do now, with the subscription.**
- Same packs, postage and packing routine as the posted subscription (see [bean-subscription.md](bean-subscription.md)).
- Offer one-off bags at £14.50 posted and two bags at £26.
- Christmas: bean gift box (£24 with a £10 gift card, from [office-catering.md](office-catering.md)), posted by Wed 9 Dec.
- Grind's online business is now a larger part of its revenue than its cafés ([Shopify case study](https://www.shopify.com/blog/grind-dtc-expansion)). That is the long-term case for doing online well.
- **Rules to confirm with a solicitor:** 14-day cancellation for distance sales (perishable goods may be exempt), clear delivery charges before checkout, and a returns policy.

### B. Local delis and grocers. **Test with 2–3 stockists.**
- **Who to approach** *(no contacts named)*: independent delis and fine-food shops in St James's, Mayfair and Soho. Indonesian, Malaysian and Southeast Asian grocers in central London. Gift shops near Piccadilly that sell food.
- **Terms** *(assumption)*: shelf price £12, wholesale £7.80 ex VAT, minimum 12 bags, payment in 30 days, no sale-or-return. Offer a free tasting tray for launch weekend.
- **What they need:** a full prepacked label (name, weight, best before, Bija's name and address, roaster), a case of 6–12, and a shelf card ("Best New Coffee Shop, London Coffee Festival Awards 2025").
- **Risk:** the bean freshness window. Agree a best-before date with Kindred Forest. Swap old stock for fresh at your own cost after 8 weeks (*assumption*).

### C. Offices: 1 kg beans for bean-to-cup machines. **Offer to catering clients.**
- One office of 20 people drinks about 2–3 kg a month *(assumption)*.
- Ask Kindred Forest for 1 kg pricing and which blend suits automatic machines. Usually a medium roast; the House Blend is a likely fit.
- Monthly standing order delivered on foot within the 800 m radius ([office-catering.md](office-catering.md)).
- 5 offices × 2.5 kg × £11 = **about £140 contribution a month** *(assumption)* for little extra work.

### D. Etsy. **Optional Christmas test only.**
- 2 listings: bean gift box, and a "taste of Indonesia" set of 3 bags. Stop in January if under 10 sales.

### E. Amazon and Not On The High Street. **Not now.**
- The lowest contribution per bag, plus brand control issues and work answering customers. Revisit only if online sales pass about 100 bags a month and the brand needs reach.

### F. Hotels and restaurants. **Later, after 90 days.**
- The biggest volume, but it needs equipment, barista training and a service promise. Better after the subscription and office channels are stable.
