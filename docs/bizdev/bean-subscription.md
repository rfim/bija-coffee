# Bija bean subscription: offer, margin, forecast and launch

*Sprint 2 · Business Development · draft for Bija to approve · 29 Oct 2026*

**Recommendation:** launch a **collect-in-store** subscription in mid-November with **gift subscriptions** for Christmas. Add **posted** delivery once a letterbox-size pack has passed a test post. Collection makes £6.35 a bag and brings the subscriber into the shop. A posted bag makes £5.35 and is the bigger long-term market.

> **How to read the numbers.** Anything marked *(assumption)* is our estimate and has no source. Sourced figures have a link. The model is in [`bizdev-models.xlsx`](bizdev-models.xlsx) on the *Subscription* sheet. Yellow cells are assumptions, so change them and the forecast updates.

---

## 1. The offer

| | **Collect in store** | **Posted** |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | One 200 g bag of Indonesian beans, roasted with Kindred Forest | Same, through the letterbox |
| Frequency | Monthly or fortnightly | Monthly or fortnightly |
| Beans | Pick one, or "Roaster's choice" (rotates Sumatra Mandheling, Sumatra Lintong, Old Brown Java, House Blend) | Same |
| Grind | Whole bean, or ground for espresso, filter or cafetière | Same |
| Price per bag | **£12** (same as the shop) | **£14.50 including P&P** *(assumption: proposed price)* |
| Subscriber perk | Free filter coffee or kopi tubruk on collection day, plus double Bija Card points on the bag *(assumption: perk cost about £0.60 in ingredients)* | Double Bija Card points, plus a tasting card in the box |
| Control | Pause, skip or cancel any time in the app, at least 3 days before the next date | Same |
| Gift subscription | Prepaid for 3 or 6 months, with a printed or e-card for the recipient | Same |

**Why these choices**
- **Pause and skip, not just cancel.** Grind sells its subscription on "skip, pause, or cancel anytime" ([grind.co.uk](https://grind.co.uk/pages/roastery)). Letting people pause stops a holiday from becoming a cancellation.
- **No discount on the bag.** Grind discounts the first two orders and posts free ([grind.co.uk](https://grind.co.uk/pages/roastery)). It can afford to because it has national volume. At Bija's size a perk that costs very little (a filter coffee on collection) protects margin and brings the subscriber to the counter, where they may buy a cake.
- **Gift subscriptions** are the Christmas hook, and they pair with the existing gift cards (£5–£200).

### Gift subscription prices *(assumption: proposed)*

| | Collect | Posted |
|---|---|---|
| 3 months (3 bags) | £36 | £43.50 |
| 6 months (6 bags) | £72 | £87 |

## 2. Pricing and margin per bag

Bean cost is **45% of the £12 shop price = £5.40 per bag**. This is the assumption from the brief, because we have no Kindred Forest invoice yet. We assume it includes the printed retail bag.

| Per bag | Collect | Posted |
|---|---|---|
| Price paid by customer | £12.00 | £14.50 |
| Beans and retail bag (45% of £12) *(assumption)* | −£5.40 | −£5.40 |
| Card/payment fee *(assumption: ~2%)* | −£0.25 | −£0.25 |
| Mailer box and label *(assumption: quote needed)* | – | −£0.60 |
| Postage: Royal Mail Tracked 48 Large Letter, online | – | −£2.90 |
| **Contribution per bag** | **£6.35 (53%)** | **£5.35 (37%)** |
| Less collection-day perk *(assumption)* | −£0.60 → **£5.75** | – |

The table leaves out staff time to pack (about 2 minutes a bag, *assumption*) and VAT.

**VAT.** We understand that roasted coffee beans are zero-rated food in the UK under HMRC VAT Notice 701/14. Postage charged with zero-rated goods may also take the goods' rate. **Confirm both with an accountant** before setting the posted price.

### Two-bag posted option
Two bags will not fit the 2.5 cm large-letter limit, so they go as a Tracked 48 Small Parcel at **£3.75**. Price at **£26** *(assumption)*: £26 − £10.80 beans − £0.80 box − £3.75 postage − £0.45 fees = **£10.20 (39%)**. Offer this to fortnightly subscribers who would rather get one delivery a month.

## 3. Postage options (Royal Mail)

Royal Mail **online** prices valid from 5 October 2026, including VAT. Source: [Royal Mail online price guide, Oct 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-10/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1.pdf), also at [royalmail.com/current-postage-prices](https://www.royalmail.com/current-postage-prices).

| Service | Format and limits | Online price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tracked 48** | Large Letter, up to 1 kg, max 35.3 × 25 × 2.5 cm | **£2.90** | **Recommended.** Tracked and goes through a letterbox, for 20p more than 2nd Class |
| Tracked 24 | Large Letter, up to 1 kg | £3.90 | Only for late or replacement bags |
| 2nd Class | Large Letter, up to 750 g | £2.70 | Cheapest, but untracked, which makes "it didn't arrive" claims hard to settle |
| 1st Class | Large Letter, up to 750 g | £3.30 | Dearer than Tracked 48 and untracked |
| Tracked 48 | Small Parcel, up to 2 kg, max 45 × 35 × 16 cm | £3.75 | Two-bag orders |
| 2nd Class | Small Parcel, up to 2 kg | £4.40 | Dearer than Tracked 48, so don't use it |
| Tracked 48 with signature | Large Letter / Small Parcel | £4.90 / £5.35 | Not needed for £12 goods |

Royal Mail's printed counter leaflet ([Our Prices, Oct 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-10/our-prices-october-2026-v1.pdf)) lists higher counter prices, such as £4.70 for a Tracked 48 Small Parcel. **Always buy labels online** or through a business account. Business account rates may be lower again ([Mailmark franking wallchart, Oct 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/mailmark-franking-prices-wallchart-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf)). Ask once volume passes about 100 items a month.

**The 2.5 cm test is the key risk.** A 200 g bag with a flat bottom is too thick for a large letter. Packed flat as a pouch it can fit. Before we offer posting, post 5 test packs to staff addresses and confirm each fits the Royal Mail size guide and arrives intact.

## 4. Packaging

| Item | Spec | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Bean bag | Keep Bija's current 200 g bag. Ask Kindred Forest whether it can supply a **flat pouch (no gusset) with a one-way valve** for the posted version | Inside the 45% assumption |
| Mailer | Kraft letterbox box, fits inside 35 × 25 × 2.5 cm, recyclable cardboard, Bija mark stamped or stickered | ~£0.45–£0.60 *(assumption: get 3 quotes)* |
| Insert | A6 card: bean story, brew guide, QR code to pause or skip in the app | ~£0.05 *(assumption)* |
| Label | Royal Mail Click & Drop label, printed in store | Inside postage |

**Food labelling.** The bag already sold over the counter must show the information prepacked food needs: name, net quantity, best before, and the business name and address. Posted bags must show the same. **Confirm the label wording with a food-law adviser or Westminster Food Safety.**

## 5. Churn assumptions

We have no Bija data yet, so every figure here is an *(assumption)* to replace with real numbers after month 2.

| Assumption | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly churn (cancellations ÷ active subscribers) | **10%** | A cautious figure for a new, small subscription. Pause and skip should lower it |
| Gift subscriptions | Stop when prepaid. Assume 20% convert to paid | No source |
| Share choosing post | 40% | Many customers are office workers nearby who pass the shop |
| Monthly vs fortnightly | 75% / 25% → 1.25 bags per subscriber per month | No source |

What to measure from day one: sign-ups, pauses, skips, cancellations with a one-tap reason, and the average spend of a subscriber on collection day.

## 6. Six-month forecast

New subscribers in December include Christmas gift subscriptions, so the January cohort will drop off faster than the table shows. All inputs are *assumptions* and come from the *Subscription* sheet in `bizdev-models.xlsx`.

| Month | New subscribers *(assumption)* | Active at month end | Bags | Revenue incl. P&P | Contribution* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 2026 | 40 | 40 | 50 | £650 | £297 |
| Jan 2027 | 30 | 66 | 82 | £1,072 | £491 |
| Feb 2027 | 20 | 79 | 99 | £1,290 | £591 |
| Mar 2027 | 20 | 91 | 114 | £1,486 | £680 |
| Apr 2027 | 20 | 102 | 128 | £1,663 | £761 |
| May 2027 | 20 | 112 | 140 | £1,821 | £834 |
| **6 months** | **150** | | **614** | **£7,983** | **£3,654** |

\*Contribution is after beans, packing, postage and payment fees, and before staff time and the collection perk.

**What drives it.** About 112 active subscribers by May is roughly 3–4 sign-ups a week from the shop's own customers. The main levers are churn (at 5% instead of 10%, May has about 140 active subscribers) and sign-ups per week. The forecast assumes no paid marketing.

## 7. Launch steps

| # | Step | Owner | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get the real cost of a 200 g bag from Kindred Forest, and ask about a flat valve pouch for posting | Bija owner | 5 Nov |
| 2 | Choose how to take payment. The app runs in demo mode, so recurring card payments need a provider such as Stripe Billing or Shopify Subscriptions. **The product lead decides how this fits the app** | Product lead | 6 Nov |
| 3 | Confirm VAT on beans and postage with an accountant, and the bag label with a food adviser | Bija owner | 10 Nov |
| 4 | Write the terms: pause, skip and cancel rules, billing date, gift subscription expiry. Include the 14-day cancellation right for distance sales under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 (**confirm with a solicitor**). The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 also brings in new subscription-contract rules. **Ask a solicitor which apply and when** | Bija owner + solicitor | 10 Nov |
| 5 | **Soft launch, collect only.** QR code at the till, a card in every bag sold, an Instagram post, a staff script ("Want this bag every month? It's the same £12 and your filter's on us") | Shop team | 16 Nov |
| 6 | Gift subscriptions on sale, with e-card and printed card. **Last order date for Christmas: 20 Dec** | Shop team | 23 Nov |
| 7 | Test post 5 flat packs. If all pass, open posted delivery. If not, stay collect-only and revisit packaging | Shop team | 30 Nov |
| 8 | Posted subscription live. Book Royal Mail collection or drop off at the nearest Post Office | Shop team | 7 Dec |
| 9 | Review at 8 weeks: real churn, mix, margin. Update the yellow cells in the model | BizDev | 18 Jan |

**Stop rule.** Stop or rethink if fewer than 25 active subscribers by 31 Jan 2027, or if monthly churn is over 20% for two months running.
