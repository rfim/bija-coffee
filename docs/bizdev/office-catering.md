# Bija for offices: meeting coffee, breakfast boxes and Christmas gifts

*Sprint 2 · Business Development · draft for Bija to approve · 29 Oct 2026*

**Recommendation:** start with **corporate Christmas gifts now**. Bija already sells gift cards, so this needs almost no new kit and has a hard deadline. Run **meeting coffee and breakfast boxes** as a small pilot from January. Deliver on foot, within a 10-minute walk, before the morning rush.

### Margin check *(assumption)*
Drinks and cakes at 30% food cost, plus about £3 for cups and lids per box. That leaves roughly a 55–60% contribution before staff time. One delivery takes about 25 minutes of staff time out and back (*assumption*). **Order minimums cover this.**

### VAT (confirm with an accountant)
Food delivered and **served** at an office is usually **catering** and standard-rated. Hot drinks are standard-rated either way. Cold cakes **delivered without service** may be zero-rated. Quote all prices **including VAT** and ask an accountant which rate applies to each line before invoicing.

Bija already sells gift cards from £5 to £200. Offer them in bulk to companies giving staff or clients a present.

| **Coffee gift box** | **£24**: 200 g bag of beans, £10 gift card, brew card, Bija box | ~£9 contribution *(assumption)* |

| Logo or message | Free printed message. Company logo on the note for 50+ cards | |

**Deadlines:** order by **Fri 11 Dec 2026** for collection or walk delivery by Fri 18 Dec. Orders that need posting by **Wed 9 Dec**.

**Points to confirm**
- **Gift card accounting and VAT.** Bija's gift cards can be spent on items at different VAT rates, so they are probably *multi-purpose vouchers*: VAT is charged when the card is spent, not when it is sold. **Confirm with an accountant.**
- **Expiry and unredeemed balances.** State the expiry on the card and in the terms. **Confirm with a solicitor.**
- Buyers often ask about the **trivial benefits** rule for staff gifts. That is a question for **their** accountant, not Bija's, so don't advise on it.

## 6. Target list: types of office nearby

St James's is dense with small, high-value offices with no staff canteen, which is the kind of client we want. **We name no people or contact details.** Find buyers by role at each place. The person who usually books is the **office manager, executive assistant (EA), PA, facilities manager or events coordinator.**

| # | Type of office | Why they fit | What to offer first | How to reach them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Offices in St James's Market and the buildings around it** | Next door; their staff already walk past | Meeting set, breakfast boxes | Ask the building's reception or management team (The Crown Estate manages St James's Market) about a tenant newsletter or welcome pack |
| 2 | **Investment managers, hedge funds, family offices** (St James's Street, St James's Square, Pall Mall) | Client meetings, high standards, Christmas client gifts | Client hamper, filter box | EA/office manager on LinkedIn, or a gift box dropped at reception |

**Outreach plan for November** *(assumption: about 4 hours a week)*
1. Print a one-page A5 menu with a QR code to order. Put one in every takeaway bag for 2 weeks.
2. Walk the 800 m radius on two mornings and leave a **free tasting box** (4 cakes and a card) at 20 receptions, on offices of types 1–5.
3. Post on LinkedIn and Instagram: "Christmas gifts from London's Best New Coffee Shop 2025".
4. Follow up every enquiry within 1 working day. Keep a simple tracker: enquiry, quote, order, repeat.

**Target for Dec:** 10 gift orders averaging £300 = **£3,000 of sales** *(assumption)*. **Target for Q1 2027:** 4 repeat meeting clients ordering twice a month.
