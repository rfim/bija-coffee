*Sprint 2 · Business Development · draft for Bija to approve · 29 Oct 2026*

## 4. Decisions needed from Bija

1. **Approve the prices** in the docs: subscription posted at £14.50, gift boxes at £24 and £45, office price list, evening set at £12. Or tell us yours.
2. **Bean cost:** send the real Kindred Forest cost per 200 g bag and 1 kg bag, and confirm the agreement allows resale, wholesale and online sales.
3. **Payment for subscriptions:** agree with the product lead which provider takes recurring payments, since the app runs in demo mode.
4. **Book an accountant and a solicitor** for the points flagged "confirm": VAT on beans, postage, catering and gift cards; subscription and gift card terms; food labels.
5. **Evening pilot:** OK to check the lease with The Crown Estate and planning, and to ask staff about Thursday evening shifts from 14 Jan?
6. **Personal licence:** does the owner or a manager want to take the course in Q1 2027, to keep coffee cocktails open as an option?
7. **Who does deliveries and outreach:** about 4 hours a week in November for walking the radius, and a 07:30–08:30 delivery slot.
