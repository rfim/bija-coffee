Sprint 2 · Marketing & Growth · Draft for Bija to approve

**Goal:** make Bija known across London and grow daily customers at 2a St James's Market.

## The winter hero drink: brief

Black Sheep Coffee credits new seasonal iced ranges with record sales weeks (its iced drinks grew 227% year on year in the week ending 27 April 2025: [World Coffee Portal](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/iced-beverages-deliver-record-weekly-sales-for-black-sheep-coffee/)). We borrow the idea of a seasonal hero drink with a name, a look and a limited run. We don't borrow their tone.

- **Working name:** *Kopi Rempah* ("spiced coffee"). **[Confirm name]**
- **Build [Assumption, head barista to develop]:** a kopi susu base with arenga palm sugar, warmed with nutmeg and clove, plus a little cinnamon and ginger. Hot by default, iced on request. Lapis legit is already a spiced cake, so the pairing story writes itself.
- **Story:** nutmeg and clove are the spices the Maluku islands were known for long before coffee reached Europe. The drink tells that story in one cup. Keep the claims general, and don't promise dates or "first ever".
- **Price [Assumption]:** in line with kopi susu plus about £0.40. Bija sets the price after costing.
- **Run:** 5 Nov 2026 to 31 Jan 2027, or "while it's winter". A limited run creates urgency, but only say "limited" if it really ends.
- **Allergens:** list any additions on the menu board and in the app (the 14 major allergens).

## Rules for every post (UK)

- **Paid, gifted or incentivised posts need a clear "#ad" (or "Ad") up front**, visible without a click. This covers any creator we pay, gift drinks to, or give a discount code to. See the ASA/CAP [Influencers' guide](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html). As house policy, we also add "#ad" at the start of the caption when we use Instagram's Paid Partnership tag, so the label is clear wherever the post shows up.
- **Promotions** (welcome points, referral, any prize draw) state their significant conditions in the post itself, such as who can take part, the closing date and how to claim, and link to full terms ([CAP Code rule 8.17 guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/not-all-conditions-are-created-equal-a-significant-insight-into-significant-conditions.html), [prize draws in social media](https://asa.org.uk/advice-online/promotional-marketing-prize-draws-in-social-media.html)).
- **Claims:** use the award wording exactly as the organiser publishes it: *Best New Coffee Shop, London Coffee Festival Awards 2025* ([LCF winners](https://www.londoncoffeefestival.com/london-coffee-festival-awards/winners-lcf-awards)). No "London's best coffee", "the only…" or "first…" unless we can prove it.
- **People in shot:** written consent from anyone identifiable (template in `launch-campaign.md`). Children only with a parent's or guardian's consent, and better not at all.
- **UK English**, prices including VAT, and allergens signposted.
