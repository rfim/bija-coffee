## Three press angles

### Angle 1: The award. "The coffee shop London's coffee industry picked"

**Hook:** London's coffee trade named a small Indonesian café in St James's its Best New Coffee Shop for 2025. Eighteen months on, what has that done for an independent? And what does it serve this winter?

**Why now:** a winter menu (Kopi Rempah), the Bija app launch and Christmas in St James's all land in November. The award opens the door; the news is what's new.

**Best for:** Time Out London, Hot Dinners, Londonist, Mayfair Times, Square Meal.

**Assets:** the award in the window (`photos/space-2.jpg`), the room, Kopi Rempah being made.

### Angle 2: The founder. "From media to the coffee bar"

**Hook:** Devi Trianna came from media, not hospitality, and opened a café to tell Indonesia's story through its coffee. Within a year it won Best New Coffee Shop, and in 2026 London Coffee Festival put her on its "Ones to watch" stage.

**Why it works:** founder stories travel in trade and business press, and Devi's media background means she's comfortable on camera and on a podcast.

**Best for:** World Coffee Portal (trade), Beverage Daily (its editor shared the 2026 "Ones to watch" stage), Caffeine magazine, Vittles (essay), the Evening Standard business pages, Indonesian press (Kompas has already covered Bija).

**Needs:** an approved founder bio, a portrait, and 2 or 3 stories in Devi's own words (why coffee, why St James's, the hardest week).

### Angle 3: Indonesian origin. "Indonesia, by the cup"

**Hook:** most Londoners know Sumatra only as a word on a coffee bag. Bija serves Indonesian coffee the way it's drunk at home: kopi tubruk (grounds settled in the glass), kopi susu sweetened with arenga palm sugar, and lapis legit, the many-layered spice cake. Bija's own baristas roast the beans, from green coffee sourced with Kindred Forest, whose work supports rainforest-protecting farmer cooperatives. **[Confirm wording with Kindred Forest]**

**Why it works:** it's specific, sensory and photogenic. Baristas who roast the coffee they pour make a strong story for coffee press, and it gives food writers a reason to visit that isn't "another flat white".

**Best for:** Vittles, The Infatuation, European Coffee Trip, Standart, Sprudge, Coffee in Hand, Time Out "best coffee in London" round-ups.

**Seasonal hooks:** Hari Ibu (22 Dec), Imlek (6 Feb 2027), Indonesian Independence Day (17 Aug).

## Pitching rules

- **One outlet, one angle, one named writer.** Personalise the first line to something they've actually written.
- **Offer something real:** a tasting of kopi tubruk and lapis legit, a slot with Devi, or images ready to use.
- **Gifted visits:** journalists writing editorially don't need #ad. **Creators or influencers** who receive anything free and post about it do ([ASA/CAP Influencers' guide](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html)). Tell them that in writing up front.
- **Follow up once,** after 4 or 5 working days. Then leave it.
- **Log every pitch** (outlet, date, angle, outcome) so we don't pitch the same person twice.
