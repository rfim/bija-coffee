# Bija events calendar: 12 months, November 2026 to October 2027

A proposal for one anchor event or moment a month, each tied to an Indonesian or London date. It builds on Bija's past events (DUNIA Afterhours, Independence Day with Edge Group Conservation, Celebrasia, London Coffee Festival, the Earth Day grounds giveaway, 10% off for London Marathon runners) and sits alongside `90-day-calendar.md` and `partnerships.md`. It doesn't replace them.

**Every date below is to confirm.** Moveable dates (Ramadan, Eid, Imlek, London Marathon, London Coffee Festival) shift each year and must be checked against an official source before anything is announced or printed. Owner time is the founder's or owner's hands-on time, including planning; the café team's time isn't counted. Costs aren't estimated here.

**KPIs** are the launch four (app installs, Beans Club sign-ups, stamp-card activations, newsletter opt-ins) plus **footfall/transactions** and **bag sales**, measured against the same days the previous month. Announce every event first on Instagram (@bija.london) and in the newsletter, then list it on `events.html`.

**Owner time in total:** about 60 hours over 12 months, or 5 hours a month on average. The heaviest months are May (London Coffee Festival) and February to March (Imlek, Ramadan and Lebaran).

## Before each event

- Confirm the date against an official source and, for Ramadan, Lebaran and Imlek, with the community partners taking part.
- Check licensing for any evening event, alcohol, music or late opening.
- Write the allergen information for any food served (the 14 major allergens).
- Set a KPI target against the same days in the previous month, and review it within a week of the event.
