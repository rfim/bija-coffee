# Partnerships: outreach scripts

Sprint 2 · Marketing & Growth · Draft for Bija to approve

There are four partner groups, each with a goal, an offer, a script and a follow-up. **No contacts are invented.** Use the routes named here (official sites, the landlord's centre team, people Bija already knows) and fill in names once they're found.

**Benchmark mechanics we borrow (not voice):**

- **Grind's cultural collaborations.** Its Clueless 30th-anniversary range tied a product drop to a West End launch ([Grind](https://grind.co.uk/blogs/features/grind-x-clueless-a-limited-edition-collaboration)). For Bija, the culture is Indonesian, and the partners are local.
- **Grind's café by day, cocktails by night** (e.g. £5 Friday espresso martinis at Soho Grind: [Grind Soho](https://grind.co.uk/pages/soho-grind)). For Bija, this means **occasional evening events** like the DUNIA Afterhours format, not a nightly bar. Any alcohol needs a premises licence or a Temporary Event Notice from Westminster City Council. **[Check the current licence; assumption: none for alcohol]**
- **Black Sheep's brand partnerships** (Skate GB, built around its Robusta: [Retail Times](https://retailtimes.co.uk/black-sheep-coffee-partners-with-skate-gb-to-put-double-caffeine-robusta-to-the-test/)). For Bija, this means co-content with a partner whose story is the coffee's story: Kindred Forest.

**Before any outreach:**

- [ ] Agree a one-page "Bija in brief": the award, founder, beans, address, hours and Instagram, taken from the fact sheet in `pr-pack.md`.
- [ ] Agree what we can give: product (samples, vouchers), space (the shop before 8am or after closing), Devi's time.
- [ ] Have a short partner agreement for anything with money, logos or shared content: who posts what, approval, #ad where needed, and photo consent.

## 1. St James's Market and The Crown Estate (landlord and destination)

**Why:** The Crown Estate manages St James's Market and runs the area's seasonal campaigns: the Spirit of Christmas lights across St James's Market and nearby streets ([St James's London](https://www.stjameslondon.co.uk/news/spirit-of-christmas-lights-2025)), the advent calendar installation in earlier years ([Creative Boom](https://creativeboom.com/news/daniel-clarkes-ultimate-advent-calendar-counts-down-to-christmas-at-st-jamess-market)), and a festive-mile switch-on ([The Crown Estate](https://www.thecrownestate.co.uk/our-business/property-and-places/retail-and-leisure/property-management/property-management-events-&-offers/Festive-mile-light-switch-on)). Being in their content puts Bija in front of everyone visiting the area. They already list Bija ([stjameslondon.co.uk/places/bija](https://stjameslondon.co.uk/places/bija)).

**Route:** Bija's existing tenant or centre-management contact first. Otherwise the contact routes on stjameslondon.co.uk.

**Asks, in order of ease:**

1. Include Bija (Kopi Rempah, the app's Secret St James's trail) in their Christmas and winter social posts and newsletter.
2. A spot in the advent calendar if it runs again (e.g. a Bija Card voucher or a bag of beans as one day's prize). **Prize promotions must follow CAP Code section 8**: who can enter, the closing date and the prize all stated, and the winner chosen at random or by stated criteria ([ASA guidance](https://asa.org.uk/advice-online/promotional-marketing-prize-draws-in-social-media.html)).
3. Permission for terrace sampling on the switch-on evening (Thu 5 Nov) and any Saturday events.
4. Longer term: Bija to host a "Taste of Indonesia" morning as part of the estate's events programme, especially around Indonesian Independence Day (17 Aug).

**Email script:**

> **Subject:** Bija × St James's Market this winter: Kopi Rempah, the lights and a walking trail
>
> Dear [name],
>
> I hope the run-up to the lights is going well. I'm writing from Bija at 2a St James's Market, the Indonesian coffee shop that won Best New Coffee Shop at the London Coffee Festival Awards 2025.
>
> We've got three things this winter that we'd love to share with the St James's audience:
>
> 1. **Kopi Rempah**, our winter drink: kopi susu with arenga palm sugar, nutmeg and clove. It launches on the night of the lights, 5 November.
> 2. **The Bija app** (launching 16 November), which includes *Secret St James's*, a short walking trail of stories around the market that ends with a coffee.
> 3. If the advent calendar runs again, we'd be glad to donate a day's prize.
>
> Could we be part of your winter posts or newsletter? We can send images, copy and a short video, ready to use. And if it helps, we'd like to offer warm samples on the terrace on switch-on night, with your permission.
>
> Happy to pop over whenever suits you.
>
> Warm wishes,
> [Name], Bija · 2a St James's Market · @bija.london

**Follow-up (after 5 working days):** one line with a 15-second video of Kopi Rempah being made under the flower lamps. "In case it's useful for your posts."

**Success:** 1 Crown Estate or St James's post or newsletter mention by mid-December.

## 2. Offices nearby: corporate gift cards and catering

**Why:** St James's, Piccadilly and Haymarket are full of offices. December gifting and January team breakfasts are budget lines someone already has to spend. One office manager can mean 30 gift cards, then 30 new app members in January.

**Offer [Assumption, Bija to price]:**

| Product | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Corporate gift cards** | Packs of 10+ digital gift cards from £5 each, sent to staff in the app with a personal message, or printed codes in a batik-print envelope. One invoice. **[Confirm: can the app issue cards in bulk, and how will we invoice? See decisions.]** |
| **Team breakfast** | Kopi susu and cold brew in batch flasks, lapis legit and pastries, delivered within a 10-minute walk. Price per head **[Assumption: £6–£9]**, minimum 10 people, 24 hours' notice. |
| **Coffee tasting** | A 30-minute Indonesian origins tasting (kopi tubruk, Sumatra Lintong vs Old Brown Java) at their office or ours before opening. **[Assumption: £150 for up to 15 people]** |

**Route:** a hand-delivered card plus a 4-cup sampling box to reception or office managers. Use LinkedIn posts from Devi, and ask current regulars: "Does your office do Christmas gifts?" Only email people who've given us their address or whose business address is published for that purpose. **UK GDPR/PECR:** cold B2B email to a named person's work address is allowed if it's relevant and has an easy opt-out, but never to sole traders or personal emails without consent.

**Reception script (in person, 20 seconds):**

> "Hi, I'm from Bija, the Indonesian coffee shop in St James's Market. We've brought a few coffees for the team. If whoever looks after staff gifts or catering would like to try us for Christmas, everything's on this card. Enjoy!"

**Email script (to an office or EA contact who asked for details):**

> **Subject:** Christmas gifts for the team, from the coffee shop round the corner
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Thanks for having us earlier. Here's what we offer local offices:
>
> - **Bija gift cards for the team.** From £5 each, sent straight to their phones with your message, on one invoice. Spendable on coffee, cake or our Indonesian beans.
> - **Team breakfasts.** Kopi susu, cold brew and lapis legit, walked over warm. From £[x] per head for 10 or more.
> - **A coffee tasting.** 30 minutes on Indonesia's origins, an easy team social for January.
>
> We were named Best New Coffee Shop at the London Coffee Festival Awards 2025, and we're a 5-minute walk from you. Orders for Christmas gift cards by [date] please, though digital cards can go out up to Christmas Eve.
>
> Best,
> [Name], Bija · 2a St James's Market
>
> *If you'd rather not hear from us again, just reply "no thanks" and we won't.*

**Success:** 10 conversations and 3 corporate accounts by 31 January. £300+ in corporate gift-card sales before Christmas.

## 4. Kindred Forest: co-content

**Why:** Kindred Forest sources green coffee and cacao with a rainforest-regeneration mission. Its Indonesian coffees include Kerinci (Sumatra), with the ALKO farmer cooperative near Kerinci National Park, and coffees grown with conservation partners in Java ([Green Coffee Collective](https://greencoffeecollective.com/pages/partner/kindred-forest), [Speciality & Fine Food Fair](https://www.specialityandfinefoodfairs.co.uk/news/new-sustainable-brand-kindred-forest-debut-rare-wild-chocolate-rainforest-products-speciality-fine-food-fair-2025)). Co-content gives Bija proof behind "proud of Indonesian origin", and gives Kindred Forest a London café showcase. It's the Black Sheep × Skate GB mechanic: a partner whose story *is* the product.

**The relationship (confirmed by Bija, 29 Oct 2026):** Kindred Forest sources the green coffee, and Bija's own baristas roast it. Copy must say exactly that, never "roasted by Kindred Forest". Still to confirm: which of Bija's four beans come through Kindred Forest.

**Content ideas (one a month):**

| Piece | Format | Who leads |
|---|---|---|
| "From Kerinci to St James's": where the coffee grows and who grows it | IG collab carousel + Reel using Kindred Forest's origin footage (with their permission and credit) | Kindred Forest supplies the footage; Bija edits |
| Cupping at Bija: Kindred Forest and the Bija baristas who roast the coffee taste the range, open to Bija Card members | In-store event, early 2027; Reels from it | Both |
| A single-origin guest bean, for a limited time, sourced with Kindred Forest | Shelf, app and Origins Passport stamp | Bija |
| Earth Day 2027 (22 Apr): coffee-grounds giveaway plus a rainforest story | Repeat of Bija's past event, with Kindred Forest co-hosting | Both |

**Rules:** each party approves the other's posts. Every environmental claim ("regenerating rainforests", "protects the national park") must be **Kindred Forest's own, with evidence**. Phrase it as theirs ("Kindred Forest works with…"), never as Bija's general claim. This follows the CMA Green Claims Code ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/green-claims-code-making-environmental-claims)) and the CAP Code rules on environmental claims. If any money or free product changes hands for the posts, label them accordingly.

**Email script:**

> **Subject:** Bija × Kindred Forest: telling the origin story together
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Bija's customers ask us about origin every day, and the honest answer is your work: the farmers and forests behind the beans. We'd love to tell that story properly, with you.
>
> A few ideas for this winter and spring:
>
> 1. A joint Instagram post and Reel, "From Kerinci to St James's", using your origin footage (credited) and our bar.
> 2. A members' cupping at Bija in early 2027.
> 3. A limited guest bean sourced with you, with a stamp in our app's Origins Passport.
> 4. Co-hosting our Earth Day coffee-grounds giveaway in April.
>
> We'd run every claim past you before anything goes out. Could we find 30 minutes in the next couple of weeks?
>
> Best,
> [Name], Bija · 2a St James's Market · @bija.london

**Success:** 1 collab post published in January (week 12 in the calendar). The cupping date agreed.
