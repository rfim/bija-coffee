## 1. What we're launching (as built in this repo)

These details are checked against `app.html` on this branch:

- **Order ahead:** choose a collection slot (ASAP, 10, 20 or 30 min) and skip the queue.
- **Bija Card points:** 10 points per £1. Rewards: **350 = any cake · 400 = any drink · 1,200 = a 200 g bag of beans**.
- **Welcome bonus: +100 points** on joining (`BONUS.welcome = 100`).
- **Challenges:** *Regular* (3 visits in a week, +150), *Explorer* (5 different drinks, +150), *Birthday* (+200). Together these are capped at **200 points per member per calendar month**.
- **Origins Passport:** stamp all five origins for +600 (Lintong, Mandheling, Gayo, Kintamani, Old Brown Java). **[Confirm]** Gayo and Kintamani are in the app's passport but are not among the four beans on the shelf. Do we serve them as filter or guest coffees?
- **Secret St James's** walking trail: +400 when complete.
- **Gift cards** from £5, sent to someone's app.

> ⚠️ **Referral is not in the app yet.** The brief asks for a referral offer, so a proposed spec is in §3 for the product lead. Until it ships, all launch materials leave referral out. Swap in the referral versions (marked **[R]**) only once it is live.

## 2. The message

**Benchmark mechanic, not voice.** Black Sheep Coffee anchors everything on one bold line ("Leave the herd behind"). We borrow the idea of a single, repeatable line in Bija's own voice: warm, proud of origin, precise about coffee.

**Brand line options [Bija to choose]:**

1. **"Indonesia, by the cup."** *(recommended: short, proud, works on a sleeve, in a caption and in a press headline)*
2. "From the islands to St James's."
3. "Coffee with a homeland."

**Launch headline:** *Your Bija, in your pocket.*
**Support line:** *Order ahead, collect points on every cup, and earn a free drink at 400. Join today and we'll start you with 100 points.*

**Message hierarchy (in this order on every piece):**

1. Skip the queue: order ahead from your desk.
2. Every £1 = 10 points, and a free drink at 400.
3. +100 points when you join.
4. *(Then the story:)* the Origins Passport, the Secret St James's trail and gift cards.

**Tone check:** say "kopi susu with arenga palm sugar", not "sweet latte". Name the origin (Sumatra Lintong, not "Indonesian coffee"). Be warm, never hype. No exclamation-mark pile-ups.

## 3. The offer

### Welcome: +100 points (live)

On materials: **"Join the Bija Card and get 100 points. New members only, one per person. Points have no cash value. Terms: [terms URL]."**
100 points is a quarter of the way to a free drink. Say that plainly; don't dress it up as a "free gift".

### Referral: proposed spec for the product lead [Decision needed]

| | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Mechanic | Each member gets a personal invite link in the app. |
| Friend gets | The usual +100 welcome points, **plus 100 more** after their first paid order of £3 or more. |
| Member gets | **+150 points** when that friend's first paid order is collected. |
| Limits | Up to 5 rewarded referrals per member per calendar month. The friend must be new (one account per phone or email). No self-referral. |
| Cap | Referral points sit **outside** the 200-point monthly challenge cap, so the cap doesn't quietly cancel what we promised. **[Confirm]** |
| Cost [Assumption] | At 10 points per £1, a 400-point drink is worth roughly £4 retail and about £1 in cost. One successful referral pays out 250 points (100 + 150), about 62% of a drink. |
| Why | Black Sheep's app gives new users points and the referrer a drink voucher after the friend's first purchase (reported by [Money Monday](https://moneymonday.beehiiv.com/p/money-monday-18-nov-2024); third-party source, terms may have changed). Paying on the friend's *first purchase*, not on sign-up, stops fake accounts. |

**Compliance for referral sharing:** the ASA has said posts that share referral or affiliate codes "can still count as ads, even if they appear on personal social media accounts", and that the brand can be responsible because it controls the codes ([Osborne Clarke on the Feb 2026 ASA rulings](https://marketinglaw.osborneclarke.com/advertising-regulation/asa-holds-online-pharmacies-accountable-for-customer-shared-referral-codes/)). Those rulings were about prescription medicines, but the principle carries over. So:

- the app's prefilled share message starts with **"Ad: I get Bija Card points if you join with my link."**
- referral terms tell members to keep that label if they post the link publicly.
- we never ask members to post the link on social media; sharing with friends directly is the point.
