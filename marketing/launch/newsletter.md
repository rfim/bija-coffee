Hello,

Since we opened at 2a St James's Market, you've asked how to take Bija home. Today there are a few new ways.

## The web shop and the Beans Club
Our coffees are now online: Sumatra Mandheling, Sumatra Lintong, Old Brown Java and the House Blend, roasted with our partner Kindred Forest. Join the Beans Club for **10% off every bag**, delivered every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
[Shop coffee → LINK: shop.html]

## The Bija Card
Collect points, plus a stamp with every drink. **Your 10th drink is free.** It lives in the Bija app; show it at the till.
[Get the app → LINK: app.html]

## The Bean passport
Scan the QR on your bag or cup to see where your coffee comes from. Aceh Gayo and Bali Kintamani are coming soon, so there's more to explore.

## Secret St James's
A walking trail in the app. Pick up a kopi susu, open the trail and see the neighbourhood differently.

## Still the café you know
Kopi susu with arenga palm sugar, kopi tubruk, cold brew, the Arenga Affogato and lapis legit, at the place that was named Best New Coffee Shop at the London Coffee Festival Awards 2025.

See you soon,
Devi and the Bija team
