# Newsletter copy blocks

Reusable blocks for the email tool. Each one is a heading, one or two sentences and a button. Mix them for the launch email (see `newsletter.txt`) and the follow-ups in `plan.md`. Images are the black and white graphics in `assets/` or real café photos, and **text always sits outside the photo**, never on top of it.

**Footer (every send):** the terms line, company details, why they're getting it, a one-click unsubscribe and a privacy link. Copy it from the bottom of `newsletter.txt`.
