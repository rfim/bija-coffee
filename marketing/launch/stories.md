# Instagram Stories: 6 scripts

Format: 1080 × 1920. Keep words and stickers between 250 px from the top and 250 px from the bottom (the Instagram UI covers the rest). Each script is 3–4 frames. The last frame of each uses the matching graphic from `graphics/png/story-*.png`.

Rules: never put text or stickers over café photos or video of the café. Use a plain black or white frame, or the graphic, for any words. Add captions to any spoken video (Instagram's captions sticker), and write alt text for each frame in Accessibility settings.
