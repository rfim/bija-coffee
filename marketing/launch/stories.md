# Instagram Stories: 6 scripts

**Format:** 1080 × 1920. Each Story opens on its graphic from `assets/story-*.svg`. Real footage follows on the next frames, with captions **beside or below** the footage (Instagram's caption sticker on a plain band), never written over the café photo itself.
**Length:** 3 to 5 frames, about 15 seconds each. Add a link sticker on the last frame.

---
