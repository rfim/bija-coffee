# 4-week London launch plan: website, app, Bija Card and Beans Club

**Dates:** Mon 16 Nov to Sun 13 Dec 2026 (fits the 90-day calendar in `docs/marketing/90-day-calendar.md`).
**Prep week:** w/c 9 Nov. Print materials, generate the QR codes, soft-launch the app to regulars, and schedule posts 1 to 3.

**Owners (roles; Bija assigns names):** **F** = Founder (Devi) · **ML** = marketing lead · **SM** = shop manager · **B** = barista on shift · **PL** = product lead
**Owner time** is the extra time the launch takes that day, on top of normal shifts. All times are **estimates [Assumption]**.

## KPIs

| KPI | How we count it | 4-week target [Assumption, reset after week 1] |
|---|---|---|
| **App installs** | New Bija app accounts (Barista view → Members), split by UTM source | 600 |
| **Beans Club sign-ups** | New active subscriptions in the online shop | 40 |
| **Stamp-card activations** | Members with at least one Origins Passport stamp | 250 |
| **Newsletter opt-ins** | New subscribers with consent (footer form and app) | 400 |
| *Guardrail* | Share of bar transactions with a Bija Card QR scanned | 25% by week 4 |

**Weekly review:** every Monday at 8.30am, 20 minutes (F, ML, SM). Look at the four KPIs, the best and worst post and the best QR source, then keep going or change one thing.
