# Bija London launch plan: 4 weeks

The goal is to get regulars and new visitors in St James's onto the new features: the web shop and Beans Club, the Bija Card stamp card, the Bean passport and Secret St James's.

D0 is launch day. Days run D0 to D27. "Owner min" is the time the owner needs to spend that day; the team covers the rest at the till.

## KPIs

Track daily in one shared sheet. Review every Sunday (D6, D13, D20, D27).

| KPI | Where it comes from | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 | 4-week total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| App installs | App store consoles, or PWA installs from analytics | 150 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 450 |
| Beans Club sign-ups | Web shop subscriptions | 20 | 15 | 15 | 20 | 70 |
| Stamp-card activations | Bija Cards with a first stamp | 200 | 150 | 150 | 150 | 650 |
| Newsletter opt-ins | Newsletter list, new consented sign-ups | 80 | 60 | 60 | 60 | 260 |

<!-- These targets are starting guesses, not from Bija data. Reset them at the D6 review based on week 1 footfall. -->

Supporting signals: Instagram reach and saves per post, link-in-bio clicks, QR scans per poster or table talker (use a different QR link for each so you can see which one works), and Secret St James's trail starts.

## Before D0 (setup, about 3 hours in total)

- Print the till cards, table talkers and QR poster (`in-store.md`). Test every QR on iPhone and Android.
- Brief the team on the till script: offer the Bija Card with every order.
- Schedule posts 1–3 and Story 1. Load the newsletter (`newsletter.md`) and send a test to yourself.
- Set up the KPI sheet with a column per day.
