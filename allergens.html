Tell us before you order

If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please tell our team before you order, in the café or in the note on your app order. We'll show you the full allergen information for every drink and cake, from our recipe sheets and supplier labels, and we'll tell you honestly if we can't be sure.

We make drinks and serve cakes in one small kitchen and bar, so we can't promise any item is completely free from an allergen. Cakes may contain traces of nuts, milk, gluten and egg.

The 14 major allergens

UK law (the Food Information Regulations 2014) asks us to tell you if any of these are in our food and drink:

  • Celery
  • Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats)
  • Crustaceans
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Lupin
  • Milk
  • Molluscs
  • Mustard
  • Tree nuts
  • Peanuts
  • Sesame
  • Soya
  • Sulphur dioxide and sulphites

Milk alternatives

We offer oat, soya and coconut milk at no extra cost. Soya milk contains soya. Oat milk may contain gluten unless the label says otherwise; ask us which brand we're using today. Our steam wand is cleaned between drinks, but the same jug and wand are used for all milks.

Coffee beans

Our 200 g bags contain only roasted coffee beans. They are packed by our roasting partner; check the label on your bag for any "may contain" statement.
