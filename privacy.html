Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Draft for Bija Coffee Limited to review with its adviser before launch. It describes how this website and app work today.

Who we are

Bija Coffee Limited (company number 15383542), 2a St James's Market, London SW1Y 4AH, is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about your data at info@bijalondon.co.uk.

We do not ask for your email address, phone number or date of birth in the app, and we do not sell your data. If you email us (for example to register interest in the bean subscription), we use your email address only to reply.

Who can see it

Bija staff who run the bar and the owner can see orders, points and gift cards so they can serve you. Your data is stored with our app hosting provider, which processes it on our behalf. Card payments, when added, will be handled by a regulated payment provider; we will not see your full card number.

How long we keep it

Orders and loyalty records are kept while your Bija Card is active and for up to 2 years after your last visit, then deleted. Gift card records are kept for 6 years for accounting. Finance records are kept as UK tax law requires.

Cookies and storage on your device

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The app saves a few settings in your browser's storage so it works the way you left it: whether you prefer the website or the app, your card design, your last drink options, and whether you dismissed the install tip. These are strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so we don't need to ask for consent, but you can clear them at any time in your browser settings. The website uses fonts already on your device and loads nothing from other companies. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to our use of it, or ask us to restrict it. Email info@bijalondon.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
