We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising profiles. Staff records the owner keeps for payroll (first name, hours, pay rate) are covered by our separate notice for employees.

Who can see it

Bija staff who run the bar and the owner can see orders, points, subscriptions and gift cards so they can serve you. Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in its London (UK) data centre; it processes the data only on our behalf, under a contract. When you use the app we give it an anonymous account ID, so we don't need your email to keep your Bija Card. Parcels are sent with Royal Mail, which receives your name and address to deliver them. Card payments, when added, will be handled by a regulated payment provider; we will not see your full card number. Email news, if you opt in, is sent through an email provider acting on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Orders, subscriptions and loyalty records are kept while your Bija Card or subscription is active and for up to 2 years after your last order or visit, then deleted. Sales, gift card and finance records are kept for 6 years, as UK tax law requires. Your news email is kept until you unsubscribe.

Cookies and storage on your device

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and this site sets no cookies at all. The website and app save a few things in your browser's storage so they work: your basket, orders you placed in this browser, whether you prefer the website or the app, your card design, your last drink options and whether you dismissed the install tip. These are strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations we don't need to ask first, but you can clear them at any time in your browser settings. Our fonts are stored on our own site, so no third party learns that you visited.

Camera

If you tap "Scan coffee" in the app, your phone asks permission to use the camera. The camera picture stays on your phone: it is only used to read the QR code on your bag or cup, nothing is recorded or sent to us, and the camera turns off when you close the scanner.

Children

The Bija Card is meant for people aged 13 and over. If you're younger, please ask a parent or carer to order for you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to our use of it, or ask us to restrict it. Email info@bijalondon.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
